# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
The public homepage and listing pages serve people in Vietnam who are searching for rooms, comparing prices and locations, and trying to decide whether a room is worth contacting or viewing. The authenticated management surfaces serve landlords who manage houses, rooms, listings, viewings, contracts, invoices, and reports; tenants who track room, contract, service, invoice, and viewing information; and admins who approve rooms, manage users, and monitor demand.

## Product Purpose
TTCS Quan Ly Nha Tro helps renters find suitable rooms and helps landlords operate rental housing from one connected system. The public experience should make room discovery feel trustworthy and efficient: listings need to be scannable, local, photo-led, and grounded in practical details like price, area, address, and availability. The management experience should keep daily operations calm, predictable, and fast, with clear state, standard controls, and reliable workflows.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, efficient, local/Vietnamese. The public surfaces should feel useful and grounded rather than flashy, with a direct voice that respects renters' time and landlords' need for credibility. Management screens should feel quiet, task-focused, and consistent.

## Anti-references
Avoid flashy real-estate landing-page styling, luxury-property theatrics, oversized promotional hero sections, decorative gradients, generic stock-real-estate gloss, and visual choices that make listings harder to inspect. Avoid dragging brand-page drama into dashboards: management screens should not use showy motion, marketing layouts, or decorative card-heavy composition.

## Design Principles
Prioritize the room searcher's next decision: every public listing view should make price, location, room quality, and contact or viewing actions easy to compare.

Let local usefulness carry the brand: Vietnamese copy, address structure, currency, neighborhood context, and practical rental details should feel native to the product.

Earn trust through clarity: use real imagery, accurate metadata, visible states, and restrained interaction patterns instead of promotional claims.

Separate discovery from operations: homepage and listing pages may use brand-register pacing and imagery; landlord, tenant, and admin areas use product-register density, consistency, and task-first controls.

Keep the interface efficient under repetition: common management tasks should remain compact, predictable, and easy to scan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Aim for WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-operable controls across public and authenticated surfaces. Support reduced-motion preferences, avoid relying on color alone for status, keep Vietnamese text readable at mobile sizes, and make listing imagery and map-adjacent interactions understandable with text alternatives or adjacent details.
